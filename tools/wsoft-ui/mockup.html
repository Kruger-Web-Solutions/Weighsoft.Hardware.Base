Calls I made for you

  • The weight panel follows the relays exactly: yellow for LOW, green for OK, red for HIGH, and no colour when nothing is on the scale.
  • It starts in dark mode, like the Edge. Light mode is one tap in the Menu and the board remembers it.
  • The red and yellow notes sit above the weight, so the weight and the Hi-Lo bar are never covered.
  • CONNECT shows the phone steps. The board keeps looking for its saved Wi-Fi by itself, so there is nothing to retry. The screen never shows a password.
  • GROSS, MENU and SAVE are three separate boxes with space between them.
  • The PIN pad is laid out like a phone.
  • The trade words, the plate and the audit counter keep their exact wording.
